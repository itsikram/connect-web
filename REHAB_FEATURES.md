# Recovery & Rehabilitation Support Page

## Overview

The Rehabilitation page provides comprehensive, AI-powered support for individuals working to quit smoking, drugs, and other addictive substances. It's designed as a 100% functional rehabilitation tool with professional features and daily support.

## Key Features

### 1. **Recovery Profile Setup** 👤
Create a personalized recovery journey with:
- **Substance Type Selection**: Cigarettes, alcohol, cannabis, opioids, stimulants, or other
- **Start Date**: Marks the beginning of your recovery journey
- **Emergency Contact**: Someone you can call in crisis
- **Support System**: People/groups helping you (family, sponsors, therapists)

### 2. **Days Clean Counter** 📊
- **Real-time Tracking**: Automatic calculation of days since start date
- **Daily Statistics**: 
  - Total days clean
  - Cravings logged today
  - Support messages sent
  - Next milestone countdown

### 3. **Craving Management** 🌊
Comprehensive craving tracking system:
- **Log Cravings** with:
  - Intensity (1-10 scale)
  - Trigger (what caused it)
  - Emotional state (stress, sadness, boredom, etc.)

- **Craving History**: View all cravings logged today
- **Pattern Recognition**: Identify your triggers over time

### 4. **AI-Powered Coping Strategies** 🤖

- **Context-Aware**: AI adapts suggestions based on:
  - Your specific substance
  - Available time
  - Craving intensity

- **Step-by-Step Instructions**: Easy-to-follow actions you can do right now
- **Emergency Actions**: What to do if cravings get worse

### 5. **Quick Coping Toolkit** 💪
8 built-in strategies ready to use anytime:
1. **Deep Breathing** (2-5 min) - Calm your nervous system
2. **Physical Activity** (10-30 min) - Release endorphins
3. **Mindfulness Meditation** (5-15 min) - Observe without acting
4. **Hydration & Nutrition** (Immediate) - Stabilize your body
5. **Distraction** (10-30 min) - Redirect your mind
6. **Journaling** (10-20 min) - Process your emotions
7. **Cold Water Shock** (1-2 min) - Reset your system
8. **Social Support** (Flexible) - Connect with others

Each strategy includes detailed instructions and timing.

## How to Use

### Getting Started

1. **Click "Start Recovery"** on the initial screen
2. **Select Your Substance**: Choose what you're quitting
3. **Set Start Date**: Pick your quit date or recovery start date
4. **Add Contact Info**: Emergency contact and support system
5. **Confirm**: Your profile is ready to go!

### Daily Use

#### Morning (9 AM)
- ✅ Read your motivational quote
- ✅ Check "Days Clean" counter
- 📝 Review upcoming milestone
- 💬 Chat with AI if needed

#### When Cravings Hit
1. **Log the Craving**:
   - Hit "Log Craving" button
   - Rate intensity (1-10)
   - Note what triggered it
   - Add any extra context
   
2. **Get Help**:
   - Click "Get AI Strategies"
   - AI generates 4 personalized coping methods
   - Pick one and do it
   - Use quick toolkit for instant relief

3. **Optional: Talk to AI**:
   - Go to Support tab
   - Chat with your recovery coach
   - Share what's on your mind
   - Get personalized encouragement

#### Progress Tracking
- Check **Days Clean** at top of page
- Review **Milestones** completed
- See **Health Benefits** timeline showing improvements
- Read **Craving History** to spot patterns
- Track **Statistics** (cravings, messages, etc.)

### Using Each Tab

**Overview Tab**: See your health benefits timeline, milestones, and withdrawal info

## Substance-Specific Information

### Cigarettes/Nicotine
- **Peak Withdrawal**: 3-5 day mark
- **Hardest Part**: First 2 weeks
- **Most Effective**: Coping strategies + nicotine replacement
- **Recovery Timeline**: 3 months most physical cravings gone, 1 year significantly reduced

### Alcohol
- **WARNING**: Withdrawal can be severe/dangerous
- **Seek Medical Help**: Severe withdrawal needs treatment
- **Medications Help**: Naltrexone, acamprosate reduce cravings
- **Support Needed**: Strong support system recommended

### Cannabis
- **Usually Safer**: No medical emergency like alcohol/opioids
- **Emotional Withdrawal**: Often harder than physical
- **Sleep Issues**: First 2 weeks common
- **Recovery**: Full cognitive recovery in 6-12 months

### Opioids
- **SEEK MEDICAL HELP**: Medication-assisted treatment recommended
- **Methadone/Buprenorphine**: FDA-approved medications
- **Support Critical**: Counseling essential
- **Long-term**: Recovery possible with proper treatment

### Stimulants (Cocaine, Meth)
- **Emotional Crash**: Expect depression after use
- **Motivation Low**: Takes weeks to return
- **Sleep Needed**: Allow 2-3 weeks of recovery
- **Relapse Risk**: High - intensive support needed

## Tips for Success

1. **Tell Someone**: Share your goal with someone who can support you
2. **Remove Triggers**: Clean your environment of reminders
3. **Schedule Activities**: Fill the time you'd normally use
4. **Exercise**: Physical activity is powerful - start small
5. **Track Everything**: Log cravings to understand patterns
6. **Use Support Chat**: Don't suffer in silence
7. **Join a Group**: AA, NA, SMART Recovery create accountability
8. **Celebrate Days**: Mark milestones, even small ones
9. **Have a Plan**: Know what to do when cravings hit
10. **Self-Compassion**: Struggling doesn't mean failure

## What Relapse Means

**Relapse is NOT failure** - it's information:
- Shows you what triggers need more work
- Helps you strengthen your prevention plan
- Is common - most people relapse before full recovery
- Means: Start again TODAY, not next week
- Requires: Honest look at what triggered it

## Limitations & Disclaimers

✅ **This Tool Can:**
- Support your recovery journey
- Help you manage cravings
- Provide 24/7 encouragement
- Track your progress
- Teach coping strategies

❌ **This Tool Cannot:**
- Replace professional medical care
- Manage medical withdrawal (especially alcohol/opioids)
- Provide prescription medications
- Be your only support system
- Guarantee recovery (only you can commit)

**Always see a doctor if:**
- Withdrawing from alcohol or opioids
- You have suicidal thoughts
- You have severe psychiatric symptoms
- You want to discuss medications
- You need intensive treatment

## Getting Professional Help

### Finding a Provider
- **SAMHSA Locator**: findtreatment.gov
- **Your Doctor**: Ask for addiction specialist referrals
- **Insurance**: Check covered providers in your area
- **Local Clinics**: Community health centers often have low-cost options

### Types of Treatment
- **Outpatient**: Live at home, visit for appointments
- **Intensive Outpatient**: More frequent appointments (usually 9+ hours/week)
- **Residential**: Live at facility (for severe cases)
- **Inpatient**: Hospital-level care for acute medical issues

## Maintenance (After Recovery)

After you've achieved your goal (30+ days):
- **Keep Using This Tool**: For ongoing support
- **Continue Groups**: AA, NA, SMART Recovery
- **Stay Active**: Exercise remains important
- **Guard Triggers**: Be vigilant about avoiding them
- **Professional Follow-up**: Regular therapy/counseling
- **Medication**: If prescribed, stay consistent
- **Community**: Stay connected with others

## Troubleshooting

### "I can't stop craving"
- Are you using coping strategies when urges start?
- Do you have professional support?
- Are you in high-risk situations?
- Have you talked to someone?

### "I feel depressed"
- This is normal in early recovery
- Physical exercise helps
- Talk to your AI coach or counselor
- Consider medication evaluation

### "I'm isolating"
- Reach out to someone TODAY
- Call a helpline to talk
- Join a support group
- Use the chat feature

### "Data not saving"
- Check if localStorage is enabled
- Try a different browser
- Don't use private/incognito mode
- Data only saves if you're online first
