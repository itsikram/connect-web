# Rehab Page Quick Start Guide

## What is This?

A completely free, AI-powered tool to help you quit smoking, drugs, and other addictive substances. Available 24/7 on your phone or computer.

## First Time Setup (2 minutes)

1. **Go to the Quit Smoking & Drugs page**
2. **Click "Start Now"** 
3. **Answer 4 questions:**
   - What are you quitting? (select from list)
   - When did you start quitting? (pick a date)
   - Who can you call in an emergency? (phone or email)
   - Who is supporting you? (family, sponsor, therapist, etc.)
4. **Done!** Your recovery dashboard loads

## Using It Daily

### When You Wake Up
- ✅ Read your motivational quote
- ✅ Check how many days you've been clean
- ✅ Look at your next milestone

### When a Craving Hits (The Important Part)
1. **Click "Log Craving"** 
   - Rate how strong (1-10)
   - Say what triggered it
   - How do you feel? (stressed, sad, bored, etc.)
2. **Click "Get AI Strategies"**
   - AI gives you 4 things to do RIGHT NOW
   - Pick one and do it
3. **Optional: Chat with AI**
   - Tell it how you're feeling
   - Get encouragement
   - It won't judge you

### Quick Help Anytime
**Coping Tab** has 8 quick strategies you can use:
- Take deep breaths (2 min)
- Go for a walk or run (10 min)
- Meditate (5 min)
- Drink water
- Call a friend
- Journal your feelings
- Take a cold shower
- Distract yourself

Pick whichever sounds good right now.

### Track Your Progress
- **See your health improvements** by substance type
- **Celebrate milestones** (1 day, 1 week, 1 month, 1 year)
- **Learn what your triggers are** (stress, people, places, etc.)
- **Understand withdrawal** - what's normal, what needs a doctor

### Get Help Anytime
**Resources Tab** has:
- Numbers to call (SAMHSA: 1-800-662-4357)
- Support group info (AA, NA, SMART Recovery)
- What relapse prevention looks like

## Setting Notifications On

**Optional but helpful:**
- Get a reminder every morning at 9 AM
- Get an encouraging quote
- Keeps you thinking about your recovery

Just click the bell button.

## Emergency - What to Do

**If you're in crisis RIGHT NOW:**
- ☎️ **Call 911** (life-threatening)
- 📞 **Call 988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline)
- 💬 **Text HOME to 741741** (Crisis Text Line)
- ☎️ **Call 1-800-662-4357** (SAMHSA - any substance)

**These resources are FREE and CONFIDENTIAL.**

## Important Notes

### Alcohol & Opioids
If quitting these, **see a doctor**:
- Withdrawal can be dangerous
- Medications can really help
- Medical supervision saves lives

### What This Isn't
- ❌ Not replacing a therapist
- ❌ Not a substitute for AA/NA/support groups
- ❌ Not providing medications
- ❌ Not an emergency room

### What This IS
- ✅ Your 24/7 support buddy
- ✅ Craving management tool
- ✅ Coping strategy teacher
- ✅ Progress tracker
- ✅ Resource connector

## You Don't Need to Be Perfect

- **Struggling?** That's normal - use the app more
- **Had a slip?** Start again TODAY - don't wait a week
- **Feeling alone?** Chat with the AI or call a helpline
- **Not sure what to do?** The app will guide you

## Remember

**One day at a time.**

Every hour you don't use is a win.
Every day you stay clean is a victory.
Every moment is a chance to choose yourself.

🎯 **Your recovery is worth it.**
